Our article **"An effective pyramid vision transformer-based extended depth of focus technique for optimally focused microscopic imaging"** has been published in *The Journal of Supercomputing* (vol. 82, no. 12, 2026).

A light microscope keeps only a thin slice of the specimen in focus. The proposed method uses a pyramid vision transformer to fuse a multi-focus image stack into a single, fully focused image.

**Authors:** R. Ö. Doğan, H. Doğan, A. Yılmaz, F. S. Sezen · [DOI: 10.1007/s11227-026-08761-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-026-08761-6)
