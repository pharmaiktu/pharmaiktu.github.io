Date: 2026-06-16

Summary: Mechanism-of-action prediction from smooth muscle contractility signals and a neuro-focus fusion framework.
Tags: conference, mechanism of action, EDOF

PharmAI presented two full papers at **The 12th International Conference on Computer Technology Applications (ICCTA 2026)**, held in Vienna, Austria, on 16–19 June 2026:

- **Mechanism of Action of Pharmacological Agents: A New Deep Learning-based Prediction Model Using Smooth Muscle Contractility Signals** — F. T. Doğu, R. Ö. Doğan, H. Doğan, İ. Ay, Z. Kalha, Y. Kaya Yaşar et al.
- **A Novel Neuro-Focus Fusion Framework for Fully Focused Microscopic Imaging** — B. Aktürk, R. Ö. Doğan, Ü. Uzun, F. S. Sezen, H. Doğan

The first paper brings deep learning to the pharmacology bench: it predicts the mechanism of action of an agent from organ-bath contractility recordings.
