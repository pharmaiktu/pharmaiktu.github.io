Our paper **"Deep Learning-Based Image Fusion with Novel Multi-Focus Dataset for Extended Depth of Focus in Microscopy"** was presented at the **International Conference on Smart Multimedia** in Paris, France (19–21 December 2025).

The work introduces a new multi-focus microscopy dataset and a deep learning-based fusion approach built on it.

**Authors:** R. Ö. Doğan, F. T. Doğu, N. Santos De Oliveira, I. Kannada, H. Doğan, F. S. Sezen
